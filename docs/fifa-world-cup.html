Flagship Project

FIFA World Cup 2026: Real Tournament Efficiency & Breakout Performance

Every player, club, and statistic here is real — pulled from FBref's official 2026 World Cup "Player Standard Stats" table, covering 1,039 players across all 48 national squads. Nothing is simulated. This isn't a valuation project: the real data available for this tournament covers playing time, goals, assists, and discipline, but not market value, defensive actions, or physical tracking — so instead of forcing a story the data can't support, this project asks a question it actually can answer: who delivered, relative to the chance they were given?

Machine Learning

Predicting Player Output — A Real Regression

Predicting a RATE

Per-90 goal contribution from age, position, and starts ratio. Per-90 rates are built to remove the effect of playing time, so this is the harder version of the question.

A negative R² means the model does worse than just guessing the average — a real, useful finding: age, position, and starts ratio carry essentially no signal for pure efficiency.

Predicting a TOTAL Reframed

Raw goal contributions (not divided by minutes), with matches played, starts, and nineties as features — playing time is allowed to explain playing time.

R² ≈ 0.2 is the honest ceiling for this feature set: playing time and role explain a real, moderate share of output — clearly better than guessing, clearly not a finishing-quality predictor.
