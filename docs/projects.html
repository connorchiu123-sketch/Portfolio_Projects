FIFA World Cup 2026: Real Tournament Efficiency & Breakout Performance

A research notebook built on real, official 2026 World Cup player statistics from FBref (1,039 players, 48 squads) — not simulated data. It ranks goal-contribution efficiency per 90 minutes, surfaces breakout U21 performers and bench-impact players, and builds an honest regression showing what age, position, and playing time can (and can't) predict about a player's output.

pandas · numpy · matplotlib · seaborn · scikit-learn · Chart.js

Superstore Analysis: Excel & Power BI

The same 9,994-order Tableau Sample Superstore dataset (4 regions, 3 categories, 17 sub-categories), analyzed two ways: an Excel pivot-table workbook and a five-page Power BI report with DAX measures. Both are recreated in Chart.js with working filters, and both agree on the headline finding — orders discounted 20%+ lose money in every single year of the data. Switch between the two versions with a tab right on the page.
